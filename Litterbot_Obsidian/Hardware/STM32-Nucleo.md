# STM32L4A6ZG Nucleo

**MCU:** STM32L4A6ZG
**Board:** NUCLEO-144
**Package:** LQFP144

## Specifications

- Core: ARM Cortex-M4 @ 80 MHz
- Flash: 1 MB
- RAM: 320 KB
- GPIO: 114 pins
- Timers: TIM1-5, TIM8, TIM15-17
- UART: 6 channels

## Development

**IDE:** STM32CubeIDE
**Debugger:** ST-LINK (on-board)
